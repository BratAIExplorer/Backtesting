# India: SEBI and other studies on retail intraday/F&O trading vs holding

Method note: SEBI's own pages (sebi.gov.in) could not be fetched; Business Standard returned HTTP 403. Every number below comes from press/aggregator coverage that quotes SEBI. Items marked UNVERIFIED are secondary-only or conflict. No FY19 or FY22 loss % for the cash intraday study was found.

## 1. SEBI intraday study, individual traders, equity cash segment (FY19, FY22, FY23)

### Takeaway
SEBI (Department of Economics and Policy Analysis, released 24 Jul 2024) found 71% of individual intraday cash traders lost money in FY23, rising to 80% for very frequent traders. Costs add roughly 57% of losses on top for loss-makers. Per-year FY19/FY22 loss rates and an overall average loss were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Scope: FY2018-19, FY2021-22, FY2022-23; sample = individual clients of top-10 brokers, ~86% of individual equity-cash client count in FY23; peer-reviewed by a working group (academia, brokers, experts). — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html); [Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-made-losses)
- "7 out of 10 individual intraday traders (71%) in equity cash segment incurred net loss in FY 2022-23"; so about 29% were profit-makers (derived, not stated by SEBI). — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html)
- Frequency: loss-makers rose to 80% for traders with more than 500 trades a year; loss-makers traded more on average than profit-makers (no counts given). — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html)
- Costs: loss-makers spent an additional 57% of their trading losses as trading costs; profit-makers spent 19% of their trading profits on costs (FY23). — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html)
- Age: 76% of under-30 traders were loss-makers; under-30s were 48% of intraday traders in FY23 vs 18% in FY19. 54% of traders with ~3 years' experience were loss-makers. — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html); [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/key-findings-from-sebis-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment/170633)
- Large traders (over Rs 1 crore annual intraday turnover): 76% loss-makers; average loss-maker lost Rs 74,575, average profit-maker gained Rs 89,172, average net result a loss of Rs 34,977. — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/key-findings-from-sebis-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment/170633)
- Concentration: bottom 78% of traders = under 1% of intraday turnover; top 6% = over 90% of turnover. — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/key-findings-from-sebis-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment/170633)
- Participation: individual intraday traders 14.9 lakh (FY19), 78.3 lakh (FY22), 68.9 lakh (FY23) (as reported in one search summary; another outlet gives 1.5 million to 6.9 million for top-10 brokers, same data rounded). About 1 in 3 equity-cash individuals also trade intraday. — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses-sebi-study/74733.html); [Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/learning-centre/2024/7/sebi-study-reveals-key-insights-on-individual-intraday-traders-in-india)
- "Average loss Rs 5,371" for FY23 appeared in one search summary (attributed to Deccan Chronicle / Taxscan type coverage). — UNVERIFIED; I could not open a page stating it. Treat as unconfirmed.

### Inferences
- Loss odds rise with trade count and with costs; roughly a quarter to 30% profit, but profitability is concentrated in a small high-turnover tail.
- Even profitable intraday traders give up ~19% of profit to costs, a drag a backtest must model.

### Gaps
- FY19 and FY22 loss-maker % (study covered them; no source found).
- "% consistently profitable across years" for the cash segment: not found. (The only consistency stat found is for F&O, section 2.)
- Overall average loss across all traders: not confirmed.

## 2. SEBI F&O studies (FY22, FY19 base, FY22-FY24, FY25, FY26)

### Takeaway
SEBI F&O studies show 87-93% of individuals lose each period, with yearly aggregate net losses of about Rs 75,000 to 1.1 lakh crore and transaction costs of roughly Rs 25,000 crore a year. Losses persist year to year.

### Cited Findings
- FY22 study (SEBI, 25 Jan 2023): 89% of individual equity F&O traders lost money, average loss Rs 1.1 lakh; up from 87% in FY19. Only 11% profitable, average profit Rs 1.5 lakh. Loss-makers paid an extra 28% of net losses as transaction costs; even profit-makers paid 15-50% of profits as costs. Sample: top-10 brokers, 67% of individual F&O turnover. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/loss-makers-of-dalal-street-9-out-of-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-in-fy22-says-sebi-367628-2023-01-25); [Stable Investor](https://stableinvestor.com/2023/01/89-traders-always-lose-money-sebi.html)
- FY22-FY24 study (SEBI PR No. 22/2024, 23 Sep 2024): 93% of over 1 crore individual F&O traders lost money over the three years; average loss about Rs 2 lakh per trader including costs; aggregate individual losses over Rs 1.8 lakh crore; top 3.5% (about 4 lakh traders) averaged Rs 28 lakh loss; only about 1% earned over Rs 1 lakh profit after costs. FY24: proprietary traders' gross profit Rs 33,000 crore, FPIs Rs 28,000 crore (96-97% via algos), while individuals lost over Rs 61,000 crore before costs. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/93-of-1-crore-fo-traders-saw-rs-2l-avg-loss-while-top-3-5-faced-rs-28l-avg-loss-incl-costs-sebi/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/fo-frenzy-over-93-retail-investors-lost-average-rs-2-lakh-in-three-years-says-sebi)
- FY25 study (SEBI, Jul 2025): over 91% of individuals lost money; net losses Rs 1,05,603 crore in FY25 vs Rs 74,812 crore in FY24 (+41%), after costs; average loss about Rs 1.1 lakh vs Rs 86,728 in FY24; 13 brokers, ~96 lakh unique traders; unique individual traders fell from 61.4 lakh (Q1 FY25) to 42.7 lakh (Q4 FY25). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html) (via search summary; page itself 403); [Value Research](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stories/225398/average-trader-lost-rs-1-1-lakh-fo-fy25/)
- FY25-FY26 study (SEBI, 20 Aug 2026): FY26 87.7% net loss-makers (82.1% before costs, so costs flip ~5.6 points of traders); FY25 revised 90.9%. Aggregate individual net loss Rs 91,685 crore (FY26) vs revised FY25 Rs 1,11,788 crore (differs from the Rs 1,05,603 crore in the Jul 2025 release; do not mix). Transaction costs about Rs 24,859 crore (FY26) vs Rs 24,827 crore (FY25); about Rs 1 lakh crore cumulative FY22-FY26. Gross trading P&L of individuals about -Rs 72,000 crore. Average net loss about Rs 1.17 lakh (FY26). Options = about 92% of individuals' aggregate losses. Costs include brokerage, exchange charges, STT, GST, stamp duty, SEBI fees; STT from individuals rose from about Rs 1,291 crore (FY22) to Rs 6,645 crore (FY26). — [Finnovate](https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/sebi-fno-trader-losses-fy26-individual-derivatives); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/92-percentage-of-aggregate-losses-incurred-by-individuals-are-from-options-trading-sebi-study/81429.html); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-futures-options-study-transaction-costs-traders-losses-fy26)
- Persistence (FY26 study): about 90% of traders who lost in two consecutive years and kept trading lost again the next year; about 85% of trader-quarters were loss-making (about 15% profitable). — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1072039)
- Conflicts: FY26 active individual traders reported as 7.86 mn (detailed study) vs 8.75 mn (SEBI summary) vs 87.5 lakh (Finnovate); FY25 average loss "about Rs 1.14 lakh" (Finnovate) vs "Rs 1.1 lakh" (Jul 2025 coverage). Per-trader averages do not reconcile with aggregate/trader count (Finnovate caveat). — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/92-percentage-of-aggregate-losses-incurred-by-individuals-are-from-options-trading-sebi-study/81429.html)

### Inferences
- Costs of about Rs 25,000 crore a year are 25-35% of annual individual net loss; gross losses remain large (about Rs 72,000 crore in FY26), so costs aggravate rather than cause the loss.
- Winners are rare and persistent losing is the norm; the F&O edge sits with algo-driven prop desks and FPIs.

### Gaps
- No yearly FY23 loss % for F&O (the FY22-FY24 study gave only a 3-year 93%); FY23 and FY24 standalone loss-maker % were not found.
- Brokerage/STT/exchange-charge split by year: only STT FY22 vs FY26 found.
- SEBI primary documents not read; all numbers via secondary quotes.

## 3. Delivery/long-term investors vs traders in India

### Takeaway
No SEBI or NSE study directly comparing delivery holders' returns with traders' was found. Evidence available is indirect: index rolling-return data and AMFI holding behaviour.

### Cited Findings
- AMFI (data as of Jun 2021): over 55.6% of retail investors stayed in equity funds for more than two years. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/55-retail-investors-stay-put-in-equity-funds-for-over-two-years-amfi-121082001077_1.html) (snippet only)
- Business Standard opinion: Nifty 50 about 11% a year since launch including dividends; some 10-year windows had cumulative returns under 48%; median annualised 5- and 10-year returns range 9-13%; some 10-year windows were flat. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/opinion/long-term-equity-returns-claim-vs-reality-117091301487_1.html) (2017 piece; old)
- ISB study using NSE trade data on about 25 lakh retail investors (2005-06) concluded retail traders tended to lose money even in a bull run. — [ISB Canvas](https://newsletters.isb.edu/isbcanvas/Volume02/Issue08/reflections2012.html) (snippet only; outside 2019-2026)
- DSIJ rolling-CAGR analysis of Nifty: volatility falls with holding period; minimum 13-year return about 11%. — [DSIJ](https://insights.dsij.in/dsijarticledetail/miraculous-assured-returns-by-treating-equity-as-long-term-investment-8664) (snippet; not NSE)

### Inferences
- Time in market reduces dispersion, but the evidence here is index-level, not investor-level. Do not claim delivery investors earn the index.

### Gaps
- No SEBI/NSE study of delivery-investor returns; no SIP investor return study found; no academic India paper comparing holding with trading found.

## 4. Time in market, missing best days, mid/small-cap

### Takeaway
Missing a handful of best Nifty days roughly halves long-run outcomes; mid-cap and small-cap indices lose money in a meaningful share of 1-year windows but rarely over 5 years. All are fund-house or media figures, not official studies.

### Cited Findings
- Nifty 50 TRI: Rs 10 lakh invested 2005 to 2025 grew to about Rs 1.43 crore; missing the 10 best days cut it to about Rs 67 lakh. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/invested-right-before-market-crash-history-says-you-will-still-make-money-125041400079_1.html) (via search summary; page 403)
- Seven of the 10 best days of the past 20 years fell within two weeks of the 10 worst days. — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/rs-10-lakh-invested-in-nifty-in-1999-is-worth-rs-3-crore-today-484858-2025-07-16) (FundsIndia example, vendor source)
- CAIA (USD terms, about 23-24 years): missing 10 best days about 4.7% a year vs about 8.4% fully invested. — [CAIA](https://caia.org/node/7206)
- Zerodha Varsity: Nifty 500 from Oct 2003, missing 10 days roughly halved outcome. — [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/?p=16779)
- Nifty Midcap 150 TRI rolling returns, Apr 2010 to May 2025 (Mirae Asset deck): negative 1-year windows 843 vs 2919 positive (about 22% negative, my calculation); 3-year 275 vs 3487 (about 7%); 5-year 36 vs 3726 (about 1%). Motilal Oswal (2007-2022): 278 negative of 2,947 3-year windows, 24 negative of 2,447 5-year windows. — [Mirae Asset](https://www.miraeassetmf.co.in/docs/default-source/product-presentations/mirae-asset-midcap-fund---june-2025.pdf); [Motilal Oswal MF](https://www.motilaloswalmf.com/CMS/assets/uploads/Documents/922ef-motilal-oswal-nifty-midcap-150-index-fund-presentation-september-2022-r.pdf)
- 1-year extremes: Midcap 150 TRI max 169%, min -67%, average 20%; Smallcap 250 max 175%, min -70%. Smallcap fell about 80% in 2008 and about 60% in 2020 (blog claims). — [Basu Nivesh](https://www.basunivesh.com/who-can-invest-in-small-cap-funds/) (low-quality blog; treat as indicative)
- Abakkus study (Business Today, Sep 2026): Nifty Midcap 150 fell over 20% once every 4.2 years — headline only, not opened. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/mutual-funds/story/nifty-midcap-150-falls-over-20-once-every-4-2-years-abakkus-study-555987-2026-09-17)

### Inferences
- Best-day studies are start/end-date sensitive and all vendors have an incentive; use as direction only.

### Gaps
- No official (NSE/BSE/SEBI) missing-best-days study found; no like-for-like table of loss probability for midcap and smallcap across the same horizons; no India small-cap momentum academic evidence found.
